About Me

Testing discipline, growing into cloud-native platforms.

I currently work as a Quality Engineer III at RealPage, Inc., where I test web applications end-to-end using software testing tools — catching issues before they reach production and helping teams ship with confidence.

Before that, I spent four years as a Senior Test Automation Engineer at CCUBE, where I built and maintained automation suites using TestNG and the BDD/Cucumber framework, alongside working knowledge of Maven, JMeter, basic SQL, Docker, TeamCity and Jenkins.

Alongside my day-to-day QA work, I've spent the past several years deliberately expanding my range — completing structured, mentorship-led training in Ansible (Red Hat RH294), OpenShift (DO101, DO280, DO425), Docker, Kubernetes fundamentals, AWS and Microsoft Azure. I write up what I learn as public LinkedIn articles, and I'm currently preparing for the Red Hat Certified Specialist in OpenShift Administration (EX280) exam.

I'm looking for the next step: a role where deep testing expertise and hands-on DevSecOps/cloud skills meet.
